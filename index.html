What counts as a workflow

Only new jobs count. Everything else is included.

Every spec says up front which of these it is, and you agree before we build. No surprises.

Counts toward your plan

A new workflow

One job done end to end, with its own trigger, its own outcome and its own line in your monthly report.

The test: could you switch it off without anything else breaking, and does it have its own dollars-and-hours number?

Included

An extension

New capability inside a job that already runs: the same trigger and outcome, done better or in more places.

Connecting another system never counts. Every plan connects to as many systems as you use.

Unlimited

A small change

Wording, rules, thresholds, schedules, who gets notified, turning things on or off.

Fixes and updates when an app, API or model changes are maintenance. They never count.
