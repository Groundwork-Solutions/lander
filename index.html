How it works

First agent live in six weeks

A fixed sequence, all of it inside the same monthly retainer. You'll know exactly what's shipping before each stage starts.

  1. 01

    Audit

    We sit with your team for two days and follow the work — where it queues, who unblocks it, what it costs. You get a written map of every process worth automating, ranked by hours returned.

    Week 1
  2. 02

    Blueprint

    We pick one process and spec the agent against it: what it can decide alone, what it must escalate, which systems it touches, and how we'll know it's working. Agreed in writing before any code.

    Week 2
  3. 03

    Build

    We build it on your data and run it in shadow mode beside your team — it drafts, they send — until its judgment matches theirs. Then it goes live on the easy half of the volume.

    Weeks 3–6
  4. 04

    Run & tune

    Live agents drift when your business changes. We watch the escalation rate, retrain on the exceptions, and send a monthly note on what it handled and what it handed back.

    Ongoing

What we measure

The four numbers we put in front of you

We baseline these in week one, before anything is built, then report them every month. They're the ones that tell you whether the agent is earning its retainer — and we'd run the same measurement on your business.

  • 01 Resolution rate The share of inbound work the agent handles end to end, with nobody on your team touching it.
  • 02 Time to first response How fast a customer actually hears back — measured at 2am and at Monday lunchtime, not on average.
  • 03 Hours returned Time handed back to your team each week, counted against the baseline we take before we build.
  • 04 Escalation rate How often it correctly hands over. A number that moves here is a signal to retrain, not a failure.

Straight answers

The four things everyone asks

What does this actually cost?
One monthly retainer, quoted before you commit — it covers the engineer, the audit, the build, and keeping it running. There's no build fee, no setup cost, no seat licences, and no usage billing. The number we quote you is the number you pay.
Where does our data go?
Into your own accounts wherever possible. We build inside your cloud and your existing tools, we don't train foundation models on your content, and you get an inventory of every system the agent can read from and write to. You can revoke any of it in an afternoon.
What happens when it gets something wrong?
Every agent ships with a defined escalation boundary — the cases it must hand to a human, written down before we build. Shadow mode runs it beside your team until its judgment matches theirs. And every action it takes is logged, attributed, and reversible.
Do we need someone technical on our side?
No. You need one person who knows the process well enough to say when the agent is wrong — that's the whole ask. We handle the integrations, the hosting, the monitoring, and the upkeep.

Tell us which twenty hours a week you want back.

Twenty minutes on a call. You describe the work that keeps coming back; we tell you honestly whether an agent can take it — and what it would cost. If the answer is no, we'll say so.
