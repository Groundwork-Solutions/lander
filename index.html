How it works

From audit to live agent

Four stages, agreed in writing before each one begins. Once the first agent is live, the next one starts again at 01.

  1. 01

    Audit

    Two days with your team, following the work. You get a written map of every process worth automating, ranked by hours returned.

  2. 02

    Blueprint

    We pick one process and spec the agent: what it decides alone, what it escalates, which systems it touches, and how we'll know it's working.

  3. 03

    Build

    Built on your data and run in shadow mode beside your team — it drafts, they send — until its judgment matches theirs. Then it goes live.

  4. 04

    Run & tune

    We watch the escalation rate, retrain on the exceptions, and send a monthly note on what it handled and what it handed back.

Back to 01 for the next agent — marketing, inventory, a second support agent

What we measure

The four numbers we report

Baselined in week one, before anything is built. Reported every month. They tell you whether the agent is doing its job.

  • 01 Resolution rate The share of work the agent handles end to end, with nobody on your team touching it.
  • 02 Time to first response How fast a customer hears back — measured at 2am and Monday lunchtime, not on average.
  • 03 Hours returned Time handed back to your team each week, against the baseline we take before we build.
  • 04 Escalation rate How often it correctly hands over. Movement here means retrain, not failure.

Straight answers

What everyone asks

What does it cost?
A one-time build, then a monthly retainer that covers running, monitoring, and refinement. Both quoted before you commit, scoped to the process — no seat licences, no usage billing. Every agent after the first costs a fraction of the first, because the integration work is already done.
Where does our data go?
Into your own accounts. We build inside your cloud and your existing tools, we don't train models on your content, and you get a list of every system the agent can read from and write to. Revoke any of it in an afternoon.
What happens when it gets something wrong?
Every agent has a written escalation boundary — the cases it must hand to a human — agreed before we build. It runs in shadow mode until its judgment matches your team's. Every action is logged and reversible.
Do we need someone technical on our side?
No. You need one person who knows the process well enough to say when the agent is wrong. We handle integrations, hosting, monitoring, and upkeep.

Tell us what eats your week.

Twenty minutes on a call. You describe the work that keeps coming back; we tell you straight whether an agent can take it. If the answer is no, we'll say so.
