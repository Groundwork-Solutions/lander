How it works

From audit to live agent.

Four stages, each agreed in writing before it begins.

01

Audit

Two days with your team, following the work. You get a written map of every process worth automating, ranked by hours returned.

02

Blueprint

We pick one process and spec the agent: what it decides alone, what it escalates, and how we'll know it's working.

03

Build

Built on your data and run in shadow mode beside your team until its judgment matches theirs. Then it goes live.

04

Run & tune

We watch the escalation rate, retrain on the exceptions, and send a monthly note on what it handled and handed back.

Once the first agent is live, the next one starts again at 01.

What we measure

Four numbers, reported every month.

Baselined in week one, before anything is built. They tell you whether the agent is doing its job.

Resolution rate

The share of work the agent handles end to end, with nobody on your team touching it.

Time to first response

How fast a customer hears back, measured at 2am and at Monday lunchtime.

Hours returned

Time handed back to your team each week, against the baseline we took first.

Escalation rate

How often it correctly hands over. Movement here means retrain, not failure.

Straight answers

What everyone asks.

What does it cost?

A one-time build, then a monthly retainer that covers running, monitoring and refinement. Both quoted before you commit, scoped to the process. No seat licences, no usage billing. Every agent after the first costs a fraction of the first, because the integration work is already done.

Where does our data go?

Into your own accounts. We build inside your cloud and your existing tools, we don't train models on your content, and you get a list of every system the agent can touch.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

Every agent has a written escalation boundary, agreed before we build. It runs in shadow mode until its judgment matches your team's, and every action is logged and reversible.

Do we need someone technical on our side?

No. You need one person who knows the process well enough to say when the agent is wrong. We handle integrations, hosting, monitoring and upkeep.

Tell us what eats your week.

Twenty minutes on a call. Tell us the work that keeps coming back and we'll tell you straight whether an agent can take it. If the answer is no, we'll say so.
