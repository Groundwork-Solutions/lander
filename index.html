how it works

first agent live in six weeks

A fixed sequence with a fixed price at each stage. You can stop after any one of them and keep what we've built.

  2. 02

    blueprint

  3. 03

the four things everyone asks

What does this actually cost?
The audit is a flat fee and stands alone — you keep the map whether or not you build with us. Each agent is quoted as a fixed build plus a monthly run fee, and both numbers are on the table before the blueprint is signed. No seat licences, no usage surprises.
Where does our data go?
Into your own accounts wherever possible. We build inside your cloud and your existing tools, we don't train foundation models on your content, and you get an inventory of every system the agent can read from and write to. You can revoke any of it in an afternoon.
What happens when it gets something wrong?
Every agent ships with a defined escalation boundary — the cases it must hand to a human, written down before we build. Shadow mode runs it beside your team until its judgment matches theirs. And every action it takes is logged, attributed, and reversible.
Do we need someone technical on our side?
No. You need one person who knows the process well enough to say when the agent is wrong. We handle the integrations, the hosting, and the upkeep. If you'd rather own it outright, we'll hand over the code and document it.

tell us which twenty hours a week you want back.

Twenty minutes on a call. You describe the work that keeps coming back; we tell you honestly whether an agent can take it — and what it would cost. If the answer is no, we'll say so.
