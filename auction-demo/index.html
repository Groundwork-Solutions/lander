The difference

Same items. A lot less typing.

A lot of listing time goes into typing the same item twice and looking things up. That's the part that goes away.

Today, by hand
  • Type each item into AuctionFlex 360
  • Type it all again on eBay
  • Look up what similar items sold for
  • Decide on a price
  • Write the title, description and every item detail
  • When it sells, go back and update both
With your system
  • Drop the photos into your portal
  • Condition notes, comparable sales and a suggested price are ready
  • Title, description and item details are already written
  • Your team checks the draft and changes anything
  • One click puts it live on eBay and AuctionFlex 360
  • When it sells, both update on their own

Step by step

What happens to one item.

Here's the camera from the video, one step at a time.

STEP 01

Your team uploads photos

Photos go into your own private listing portal. A whole batch at once is fine. Nobody types a word.

STEP 02

Condition check

Every photo is looked over. Wear, scuffs, chips and missing pieces get written down, part by part, with an overall condition.

Anything a photo can't show, like whether a shutter works, is flagged for your team to check before approving.

STEP 03

Similar items that sold

It finds similar items that sold recently, including what your own past lots went for in AuctionFlex 360.

STEP 04

A competitive price

From those sales, it suggests a price and shows you where it sits against the others, so the reason is right there.

STEP 05

The listing, written

Title, category, item details and description, written the way your listings already sound.

STEP 06

Your team approves

Drafts wait for your team. They check each one, edit anything they want, and press approve. Nothing goes live without that.

STEP 07

Live on both. Sold on both.

It goes live on eBay and into AuctionFlex 360 at the same time. When it sells on one, the other updates on its own.

Questions

Good to know.

Do we have to change how we use AuctionFlex 360?

No. It works with your AuctionFlex 360 and your eBay account as they are today. Your team keeps using both the way they do now.

What if the system isn't sure about something?

It says so. Anything it can't tell from a photo, like whether a part works, is flagged on the draft for your team to check before approving.

Can we change a draft before it goes live?

Yes. Every field can be edited: title, price, condition, description and item details. What your team approves is what goes live.

What kinds of items can it handle?

It's set up for the categories you sell. During the build we test it on your real items, so it's ready for your mix before it goes live.

What happens if something stops working?

We look after it. Keeping it running and fixing anything that comes up is part of the service.

What about photos, packing and shipping?

Those stay with your team. The system takes over the typing, the looking things up and keeping both sites in sync.
